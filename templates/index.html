Important Limitations

  • This is an educational research prototype, not a clinically validated diagnostic system.
  • The test results show substantially lower recall for the NORMAL class than for PNEUMONIA.
  • Individual predictions can be incorrect even when the model has relatively high confidence.
  • Model confidence should not be interpreted as clinical certainty.
  • The dataset and evaluation setup do not establish real-world clinical performance.
